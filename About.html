About Me

Hi, I'm Mariia.

I make small, layered pieces in pastel, watercolor, and marker — usually in short sessions squeezed between other things. I'm drawn to quiet subjects and process-heavy mediums, where the fun is in the build-up of layers as much as the finished piece.

By training I work in electronics and embedded systems, so art is where I get to work with my hands in a completely different way — no debugging required, just color and paper.

This site is a running record of that work. New pieces get added as they're finished, sorted loosely by medium on the Works page.
