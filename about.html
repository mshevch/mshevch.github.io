About Me

Hi, I'm Mar.

I create pieces in pastel, watercolor, marker, oil paint and acryla gouache. My art is marked by realistic portraits and illustrations of people, as I have always enjoyed translating real people onto a page with vivid colors.

Working with my hands allows me to connect to others through drawing or painting them.

This site is a running record of that work. New pieces get added as they're finished, sorted loosely by medium on this website. Works page.
